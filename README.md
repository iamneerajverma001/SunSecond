## ☀️ Overview

**SunSecond** is India’s first integrated **Solar Triage, Demanufacturing & Decoupled-Traceability Platform (STDD)**. Operating under the philosophy **"GIA for Solar"** (Gemological Institute of America grading trust standard), SunSecond bridges the critical trust and economic gap in solar panel afterlife management:

1. **Second-Life Reuse Stream:** Non-destructive testing (Dark I-V, AC Impedance Spectroscopy, UV Fluorescence) to grade and certify healthy decommissioned PV modules for Tier-2 agricultural, off-grid telecom, and microgrid applications.
2. **Toxin-Free Scrapping Stream:** High-yield mechanical and electro-hydrometallurgical delamination (Dual Hot-Knife/Hot-Roller + Methanesulfonic Acid Electrowinning) recovering high-purity Silicon (99.9%), 5N Silver (99.999%), Copper, and glass without toxic thermal pyrolysis or open acid digestion.

2. Encyclopedia of 11 Advanced Non-Destructive Testing (NDE) Methods

To achieve GIA-level decision accuracy without slow metrology, SunSecond fuses four physical sensing domains—choosing fast, low-cost methods whose strengths cancel out each other's blind spots.

Domain A: Electrical Safety & Dielectric Integrity (The Non-Negotiable Veto)

  1. Dry High-Voltage Insulation (Riso,dry) & Ground Continuity (IEC 61730) * How: Applies 500V–1000V DC between shorted PV leads and aluminum frame for 3 seconds. * Decision Value: Instant field veto for gross shorts, punctured backsheets, or frame faults.
  2. Rapid Conductive-Mist / Sponge Wet-Leakage (Riso,wet, IEC 61215 MQT 15 Proxy) * Why Critical: CSIRO & IEA PVPS Task 13 proved 14%–15% of panels that pass dry insulation, I-V, AND EL still fail wet-leakage safety ("Dry Pass / Wet Fail"). * Innovation: Traditional wet-leakage uses a slow, messy 10-minute water immersion tank. SunSecond replaces the bath with a conductive surfactant-wetted foam/mist pressure bed that bridges micro-cracks across the backsheet/edges in 15–20 seconds, measuring true resistive leakage ((> 40\text{ M}\Omega\cdot\text{m}^2)) after filtering out initial capacitive charging transients.
  3. High-Frequency Capacitive Transient & Partial Discharge (PD) Screening (Advanced EE) * How: Analyzes micro-discharge spikes and dielectric relaxation curves during the 1000V DC ramp. * Decision Value: Detects internal EVA delamination voids and creeping tracking paths before catastrophic ground faults occur in high-voltage strings.

Domain B: Weather-Independent Electrical & Circuit Fingerprinting

  1. Dark I-V Curve Sweeping + Active Bypass Diode Pulse * How: Treats the solar module as a giant series diode matrix in darkness/shade (using an opaque thermal blanket on a sunny roof or inside a van). A MOSFET constant-current circuit sweeps from (0) to (I_{sc}) in 2 seconds—completely immune to clouds, tilt, and irradiance noise. * Decision Value: Extracts Series Resistance ((R_s) $\rightarrow$ solder corrosion/ribbon breaks), Shunt Resistance ((R_{sh}) $\rightarrow$ PID/cell micro-shorts), ideality factor ((n)), and instantly flags dead bypass diodes (SS-R high-profit repairable) via a 0.5-second reverse/step pulse.
  2. AC Impedance Spectroscopy (AC-IS / EIS) on PV Modules (Cutting-Edge EE) * How: Superimposes a tiny multi-frequency AC sine wave (10 Hz–100 kHz) over the DC bias at the module terminals and computes the complex impedance Nyquist plot ((Z(\omega) = R + jX)) on an STM32 DSP. * Decision Value: Moisture ingress alters internal dielectric capacitance loops, while early solder-joint intermetallic corrosion shifts high-frequency resistance arcs—catching hidden degradation months before DC power drops!
  3. Spread-Spectrum Time-Domain Reflectometry (SSTDR) (Live-Array Radar) * How: Injects a low-amplitude pseudo-noise RF code directly into live, energized 1000V rooftop strings without shutting down the solar plant. Reflections from impedance discontinuities pinpoint exact fault distance via time-of-flight correlation. * Decision Value: Lets SunSecond scan a 20-panel rooftop string in 10 seconds from the inverter box to identify which exact panels have corroded connectors, arc faults, or cracked ribbons before touching a wrench.
  4. 2-Second Optical Transmission / Mini-Flash Spot Check * Why Needed: Dark I-V is blind to optical degradation (yellowed/browned EVA or cloudy anti-reflective glass coating). * How: A quick ambient reference-photodiode ratio check (or a cheap 50 ms calibrated LED flash bar inside a shade hood) estimates optical transmittance loss within (\pm 4\%)—plenty accurate for power-band sorting!

Domain C: Optical & Thermal Structural Imaging (The Visual Trust Proof)

  1. UV Fluorescence (UVF) Imaging (365 nm UV-A) (The ₹10,000 EL-Killer) * Physics (NREL / Fraunhofer Validated): Field-aged EVA glows bright yellow-green under 365 nm UV-A light. Wherever a silicon cell has a micro-crack, oxygen diffuses through the backsheet and photobleaches the EVA—rendering every crack as a razor-sharp dark line. * Decision Value: Requires zero electrical wiring and uses a standard RGB camera + ₹3,500 UV-A LED strip inside a collapsible pop-up hood. Exposes micro-cracks, EVA acetic acid breakdown, delamination, and past hotspots in 5 seconds.
  2. Active Pulse Thermography (Weather-Independent IR) * How: Injects a safe 3-second forward/reverse DC current pulse into the module while recording with an entry-level LWIR microbolometer (256×192, e.g., InfiRay / FLIR Lepton). * Decision Value: Shunted cells, broken solder joints, and failing junction boxes light up bright red indoors, at night, or in rain. Visually showing an owner a glowing 130°C hotspot on WhatsApp creates instant, zero-argument trust to condemn (SS-F) unsafe panels.
  3. Daylight Lock-In Photoluminescence (dPL) & Daylight EL (UNSW / NREL Frontier)
    • How: Modulates panel electrical state (via inverter switching or portable 20 Hz current driver) while a synchronized NIR camera subtracts OFF-frames from ON-frames to mathematically cancel out 100,000 lux of midday sunlight!
    • Decision Value: Enables contactless drone or handheld high-resolution crack mapping in broad daylight for massive utility solar farms (catching brand-new transport cracks that haven't photobleached in UVF yet).

Domain D: Polymer Chemistry & Recycling Pre-Sorting (Bridging Reuse & Scrap)

  1. Portable Near-Infrared (NIR) Backsheet Spectroscopy (900–1700 nm) + Acoustic Tap
    • How: A 3-second handheld touch sensor (e.g., Texas Instruments DLP NIRscan Nano + PCA/Random Forest ML model) reads molecular absorption bonds of the polymer backsheet.
    • Decision Value (Dual-Purpose Genius):
    • For Reuse: Instantly spots chemically hydrolyzed PET or notorious cracking Polyamide (AAA) backsheets—preventing warranty disasters 2 years later.
    • For Recycling: Automatically separates Fluorinated (PVDF/Tedlar — emits toxic HF gas if heated) from Non-Fluorinated (PET) scrap, raising scrap resale value with CPCB/C-MET refiners!

Detailed Suite Architecture & Economics

Suite 1: SunSecond Scout — The Ultra-Portable Backpack Kit (80% of Individual Bookings)

  • Portability: Fits in a 6.5 kg waterproof Pelican/hard-shell backpack carried by a motorbike technician.
  • Custom Electronics Core (PFA-1 All-in-One Box — ~₹45,000 BOM):
  • MCU/DSP: STM32H7 dual-core (runs fast FFT for AC-IS and 20 ms Dark I-V sweep).
  • Power Stage: Bidirectional programmable MOSFET current source/sink (0–60V, 0–15A) powered by an onboard swappable 48V LiFePO4 battery pack (good for 150 panel tests/charge).
  • Safety Stage: Integrated 500V/1000V DC flyback Megger insulation module (Riso,dry).
  • Trust Chip: Microchip ATECC608B Hardware Security Module (signs raw waveforms immediately—technician cannot alter grades).
  • Companion Peripherals (~₹75,000):
  • Collapsible blackout fabric pop-up frame with built-in 365 nm UV-A LED array + smartphone mount (for UVF + Dark I-V shading in broad daylight).
  • USB-C LWIR Thermal Camera (256×192, e.g., InfiRay P2 Pro / Topdon TC002).
  • Wireless calibrated Silicon Reference Photodiode + PT1000 temp clip.
  • Total Kit CapEx: ~₹1.2–₹1.5 Lakh (Easily franchisable/leasable to local certified solar electricians!).
  • Testing Economics: ~₹70–₹85 per panel (including technician time).

Suite 2: SunSecond Express-Van — Semi-Portable Commercial Triage Unit

  • Portability: Built into a compact commercial van (Tata Ace EV / Force Traveller / Bolero Pickup) with roof solar + 5 kWh LFP battery bank (100% off-grid autonomous).
  • Upgrades Over Scout: 1. SSTDR String Pre-Scanner: Plugs into inverter combiner boxes to flag bad strings before unmounting panels. 2. Rapid Surfactant-Mist Wet-Leakage Tray: Pull-out drawer from the van rear with conductive mist nozzles + 1000V automated Hi-Pot (runs Riso,wet in 20 seconds). 3. Daylight Lock-In EL / PL Imager: Modulated DC injector + cooled Si-CMOS NIR camera (950–1150 nm bandpass filter) to catch brand-new transport cracks. 4. Portable NIR Backsheet Probe: 3-second backsheet health + fluorine check. 5. Pneumatic Corner Frame-Stripper (Optional trailer): Strips aluminum frames off condemned SS-F panels on-site so one van carries 3x more scrap laminates per trip!
  • Total CapEx (Excl. Vehicle): ~₹12–₹16 Lakh (vs. ₹2 Crore for an imported MBJ Mobile Lab).
  • Testing Economics: ~₹50–₹60 per panel at 300 panels/day.

Suite 3: SunSecond Vault-Hub — Non-Portable Regional Certification & Sorting Line

  • Why Non-Portable Matters: Issuing a bankable 5-year insured warranty (SS-A+) for commercial SMEs or NABARD-financed rural microgrids requires controlled repeatability and bulk electrical matching (Impp current binning) that only a fixed conveyor hub can deliver cheaply.
  • Pipeline Stations (45 seconds total cycle time via parallel stations): 1. Automated Dry Brush & Rinse Cleaning. 2. Parallel IEC Wet-Leakage Bed (4 panels simultaneously $\rightarrow$ 15 sec effective tact time). 3. Enclosed Dark Tunnel: Single-flash Multi-Spectral LED Pulse ((\pm 2\%) output matching, ₹6L local build vs. ₹50L Class AAA) + High-Res EL Camera + UVF Camera + NIR Backsheet Scanner. 4. Smart-JBox Repair Bench (for SS-R diode swaps) & Automated Current-Binning Palletizer. 5. High-Speed Frame/J-Box Stripping Line for SS-F scrap laminates.
  • Total Hub CapEx: ~₹28–₹35 Lakh | Throughput: 600–1,000 panels/day | Cost: ~₹42/panel.

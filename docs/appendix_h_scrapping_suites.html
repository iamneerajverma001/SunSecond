1. The Engineering Objective & Global Benchmarks

To uphold SunSecond's GIA-level brand standard, our scrapping division (SunSecond Extract) must satisfy five simultaneous constraints that no single legacy recycler in India meets today: 1. Maximum Value Extraction: Preserve unbroken low-iron solar glass, alloy-pure 6063 aluminum, 99.9% silver, copper, and 6N silicon (never downcycle into ₹2/kg mixed dust). 2. 100% Toxin-Free & Zero-Emission: Strictly NO open EVA pyrolysis (emits dioxins/HF gas) and NO raw Nitric Acid / Aqua Regia baths (emits toxic brown (\text{NO}_x) fumes and acid sludge). 3. High Speed & Low OpEx: Under 60 seconds per panel for front-end stripping and delamination. 4. Right-Sized Portability: Slash 65%–85% of logistics freight by doing mechanical volume reduction at or near the site, keeping complex closed-loop electrochemistry at regional Zero-Liquid-Discharge (ZLD) hubs. 5. Audit-Grade Mass-Balance Trust: Automated electronic weighing & sensor interlocks proving 0% landfilling and 0% informal leakage.

2. Encyclopedia of 10 Clean Scrapping & Extraction Technologies

A critical real-world engineering truth: not all condemned (SS-F) panels arrive in the same physical shape. Roughly 65%–70% have intact glass (failed insulation, PID, severe hotspots, repowered dead panels), while 30%–35% have shattered glass (hailstorms, transit breakage). SunSecond routes them through a Dual-Path Delamination Engine paired with Toxin-Free Closed-Loop Electrochemistry.

Stage 1: Mechanical Front-End & Chemistry Pre-Sorting (Portable / Mobile)

  1. Automated Pneumatic Frame Puller & J-Box Shear * How: Clamps corners and hydraulically pulls the 4 extruded 6063-T5 Aluminum rails off without cutting/shredding (preserving 100% pure alloy grade) while shearing off the copper junction box & cables in < 35 seconds. * Toxin-Free: 100% dry, zero heat, zero dust.
  2. Portable NIR Polymer & Chemistry Gate (900–1700 nm) * How: 3-second optical scan isolates Non-Fluorinated PET backsheets, Fluorinated PVDF/Tedlar backsheets (prevents accidental HF gas generation), and Thin-Film CdTe/CIGS (diverted strictly to dedicated thin-film partners).

Stage 2: Delamination — Separating Glass, EVA & Silicon (Dual-Path Architecture)

  1. Path A (Intact Glass — ~70% of Volume): Precision Hot-Knife Thermo-Mechanical Peeling * Physics: A PID-controlled Joule-heated tungsten/steel blade (~280°C–300°C — below polymer pyrolysis/burning temperature!) glides linearly through the EVA layer at 40–60 sec/panel. * Output: Unbroken, crystal-clear low-iron solar glass sheet (worth ₹13–₹18/kg direct to Borosil/Saint-Gobain/AGC) + a thin 1.2 kg polymer-silicon-silver laminate sheet! * Toxin-Free: Operates strictly below EVA/PVDF thermal decomposition thresholds $\rightarrow$ zero smoke, zero dioxins, zero HF gas!
  2. Path B (Shattered Glass — ~30% of Volume): Dry Precision Roller-Abrasion / High-Pressure Water-Jet Milling * Why Needed: A Hot-Knife jams on shattered glass shards. * How: Shattered panels pass through calibrated diamond-coated counter-rotating micro-rollers (with HEPA dry suction) OR high-pressure closed-loop water-jet milling that strips shattered glass fragments cleanly off the flexible EVA/cell sheet without pulverizing the silicon wafers into dust.
  3. Advanced Alternative (Path B Upgrade): High-Voltage Pulse Fragmentation (HVPF / Electrodynamic Shockwave) * Electronics/HV Physics: Nanosecond 100–150 kV Marx-generator pulses inside a closed water tank travel along dielectric phase boundaries (glass/EVA/silicon), selectively liberating pure silver contacts and silicon grains without mechanical blade wear. (Best suited for Phase-3 centralized high-tonnage hubs).
  4. Emerging Frontier: High-Intensity Pulsed Light (HIPL) / Ultrafast Laser Debonding * Physics: Millisecond xenon/laser flash passes transparently through front glass and is absorbed instantly by the silicon anti-reflective layer ((\text{SiN}_x)), popping the glass-EVA bond in seconds without heating the bulk panel. (Watch closely for 2028–2030 Phase-3 upgrades as laser CapEx drops).
  5. Evaluated & REJECTED for India: Cryogenic Liquid Nitrogen ((\text{LN}_2)) Embrittlement & Bulk Pyrolysis * Why Rejected: Cryogenic (\text{LN}_2) consumes massive ongoing OpEx (~₹350–₹500/panel in liquid nitrogen alone—destroying Indian unit economics). Bulk thermal pyrolysis (>450°C) emits hazardous VOCs/HF requiring expensive scrubbers and violates SunSecond's zero-toxin brand promise.

Stage 3: Toxin-Free Chemical & Electrochemical Metal Extraction (Fixed Regional ZLD Hub)

  1. Green Silver Recovery: Biodegradable Methanesulfonic Acid (MSA) + (\text{H}_2\text{O}_2) + Closed-Loop Electrowinning (The Crown Jewel!) * Why It Beats Nitric Acid: Traditional (\text{HNO}_3) produces lethal brown (\text{NO}_x) fumes and hazardous nitrate wastewater. Methanesulfonic Acid ((\text{CH}_3\text{SO}_3\text{H})) is non-volatile, biodegradable, highly conductive, and emits zero toxic fumes: $$2\text{Ag} + \text{H}2\text{O}_2 + 2\text{CH}_3\ext{SO}\text{H} \longrightarrow 2\text{Ag}^+ + 2(\xt{CH}\text{SO}_3)^- + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$$ * *Electrochemical Closedoop The silver-rich MSA flows direcy into a potentiostat-controlled elerowinning cell** (pure electrical engineering!). Applying a precise DC cathode voltage plates 99.5%–99.9% elemental Silver directly onto stainless-steel cathodes (95%–99% recovery) while regenerating the MSA electrolyte for continuous reuse—creating a Zero-Liquid-Discharge (ZLD) closed loop!
  2. Dry Electrostatic & Eddy-Current Separation (Copper Ribbons, Solder & Polymer) * How: After silver leaching, dry tribo-electrostatic separators split non-conductive EVA/PET polymer flakes from conductive Copper-Tin-Lead busbar ribbons and pure Silicon fragments with >98% purity and zero water pollution.
  3. Solar-Silicon Upcycling $\rightarrow$ EV Lithium-Ion Battery Anodes
    • How: Recovered 6N solar silicon (already 99.9999% pure) undergoes mild alkaline aluminum-BSF cleaning + precision ball-milling & carbon-coating into Si-C anode precursor for EV batteries—multiplying silicon value 5×–10× over metallurgical scrap.

Why Extract-Box 20 Is a Logistics Game-Changer for India

Look at the freight physics of a 20 kg condemned solar panel at a Rajasthan or Gujarat solar park: * Unprocessed Panel: 20 kg (high air volume) $\rightarrow$ a 10-ton truck holds only ~350–400 panels. * After Extract-Box 20 On-Site Processing: * 2.3 kg Green Aluminum + 0.35 kg Copper $\rightarrow$ sold locally within 48 hours! * 14.5 kg Pristine Hot-Knife Solar Glass $\rightarrow$ shipped straight to the nearest Gujarat/Rajasthan glass furnace (e.g., Borosil Bharuch) on local flatbed trucks! * ONLY ~1.2 kg per panel of Silver-Silicon Cell Concentrate travels to the Extract-Refinery! * Result: A single 10-ton truck carries the silver and silicon concentrate of ~8,000 solar panels (worth >₹1.1 Crore in Silver + Silicon!)—slashing chemical-refinery logistics costs by >88%!

5. Electronics Engineering Instrumentation Built Into Every Scrapping Suite

As an Electronics Engineer, you don't just buy off-the-shelf steel machinery—you embed precision control & GIA-grade cryptographic auditing into every scrapping machine:

  1. PID Thermal & Force Feedback Controller (Hot-Knife Blade): * An STM32 / Industrial PLC closed-loop PID controller regulates blade temperature strictly at 285°C (\pm) 3°C using K-type thermocouples and monitors stepper-motor torque current. If torque spikes (indicating a hidden glass crack), the blade auto-retracts in 20 ms to prevent shattering and routes the panel to Path B!
  2. Real-Time VOC & Hydrofluoric Acid (HF) Gas Safety Interlock: * Low-cost electrochemical HF + VOC MEMS gas sensors mounted above the delamination hood continuously log air quality to the cloud. If a fluorinated PVDF backsheet accidentally enters the thermal zone and spikes >1 ppm, the PLC auto-halts the heater instantly—guaranteeing 100% worker safety and audit-proof green compliance.
  3. Potentiostat DC Power Electronics (MSA Silver Electrowinning): * Precision programmable constant-voltage/constant-current (CV/CC) buck rectifier maintains exact cathodic overpotential so only Silver ((\text{Ag}^+)) plates out at 99.9% purity while Copper/Lead stay separated for sequential staged recovery.
  4. Tamper-Proof Automated Mass-Balance Scale (HSM-Signed): * Every Extract-Pod, Extract-Box 20, and Extract-Refinery has integrated HX711 / Industrial Load-Cell Weighing Conveyors signed by an HSM chip. As panels enter (SS-F QR scanned) and outputs exit (Al bin, Cu bin, Glass rack, Ag-Si bag), the cloud automatically verifies: $$W_{\text{input}} = W_{\text{Al}} + W_{\text{Cu}} + W_{\text{Glass}} + W_{\text{Ag-Si}} + W_{\text{Polymer}} \quad (\pm 1\% \text{ tolerance})$$ * This mathematical mass-balance proof makes SunSecond's CPCB Category CEEW-14 EPR Certificates and Corporate Zero-Landfill ESG Reports completely impossible to fake—cementing the GIA for Solar monopoly on both reuse and recycling!
